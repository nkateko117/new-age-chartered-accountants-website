# About Us

We are a team of chartered Accountants and Data scientists united with the ambitions to help both individuals and businesses create value through commercial strategy, tax planning, analytics and technology. With a focus on individuals and small businesses, our firm delivers tailored solutions in tax preparation, accounting, and business advisory. We combine deep industry expertise with a modern approach to help clients navigate complex financial landscapes and achieve long-term success.

## Our Services

**Bookkeeping Services**
We handle your daily financial records, bank reconciliations, and accounts management, ensuring your books are accurate, organized, and audit ready.

**Payroll services**
We provide full payroll services, issuing of IRP5 for employees, payslips, SARS submissions (PAYE, UIF, SDL), monthly payroll reports and reconciliations.

**Value creation and advisory**
At New Age Chartered Accountants, our advisory services are built to empower business growth by transforming your financial and commercial data into actionable insights. We go beyond traditional compliance work—offering strategic guidance that helps you make confident decisions, identify new opportunities, and build long-term value. Our focus is on leveraging your business data as a core asset, structured and analysed in ways that are designed to endure and evolve with your business.

**Tax services**
We provide tax clearance certificates, submission of provisional tax and annual returns, VAT returns, submission of company’s income tax and personal tax, handling SARS dispute and assisting with audits.

**Financial reporting and statements**
We prepare full financial statements, monthly management accounts, forecasting and cashflow statements.

**Fundings and compliance**
We assist in preparing business plans, proposals, Due diligence and management of business compliances.

**Internal control and business processes**
We review and assess business processes and controls and assist in improving and establishing robust internal control systems. Our goal is to ensure that your operations are efficient, compliant, and secure.
