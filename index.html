Meet the founder behind New Age Chartered Accountants.

Khuliso Neswiswa Nigel, CA(SA)

Founder & Director

neswiswakhuliso@gmail.com · 066 147 6116

Khuliso Neswiswa CA(SA) is a qualified Chartered Accountant and the Founder of New Age Chartered Accountants. He holds a Bachelor of Commerce in Accounting from the University of Venda and a Postgraduate Diploma in Accounting (CTA) from the University of Johannesburg, and has been registered with SAICA as a CA(SA) since 2023.

With a career spanning both South Africa and the United Kingdom, Khuliso brings a well-rounded, globally informed perspective to the accounting profession, equipping him to support clients across diverse industries and markets with confidence and insight. He is also FMVA certified in financial modelling and valuation, and leads the firm with a commitment to integrity, excellence, and client-focused service.
