## PROFESSIONAL BIO

Khuliso Neswiswa CA(SA) is a qualified Chartered Accountant and the Founder of New Age Chartered Accountants. He holds a Bachelor of Commerce in Accounting from the University of Venda and a Postgraduate Diploma in Accounting (CTA) from the University of Johannesburg, and has been registered with SAICA as a CA(SA) since 2023.

With a career spanning both South Africa and the United Kingdom, Khuliso brings a well-rounded, globally informed perspective to the accounting profession. His experience across local and international firms has given him a broad and versatile skill set in accounting, auditing, taxation, and advisory services, equipping him to support clients across diverse industries and markets with confidence and insight.

Khuliso is also FMVA certified in financial modelling and valuation, adding further technical depth to the advisory support he offers clients. He leads New Age Chartered Accountants with a commitment to integrity, excellence, and client-focused service, and a genuine passion for empowering businesses through sound accounting, tax, and advisory guidance.
